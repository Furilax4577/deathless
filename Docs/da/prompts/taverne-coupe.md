Cutaway dollhouse view of the inside of a single large medieval tavern, roof and front wall removed, seen from a
high three-quarter angle, the whole room visible, plain neutral background around the building.

One big rectangular hall, about twice as long as it is deep, with a high ceiling of thick wooden beams and a wooden
gallery with a balustrade running along the back wall above the bar, reached by a straight wooden staircase on the
right wall. Wide double entrance doors in the middle of the near wall. Plank floor, cream plaster walls with dark
timber frame, small windows with warm daylight.

Against the back wall on the right: a long wooden bar counter with big barrels on tap behind it and shelves of
mugs. Behind the counter stands the innkeeper, a cheerful sturdy woman in a Bavarian dirndl dress with braided
blonde hair, holding several beer mugs. On the left wall: a large stone fireplace with a warm orange fire. In the
back left corner: a small low wooden stage where a bard with a feathered hat plays the lute, musical notes feeling
festive. Near the fireplace, slumped on a bench with a bottle, a scruffy ragged hobo with a patched coat and a
battered hat, the tavern's regular. One long table with benches in the left half, two round tables with stools in
the right half near the windows.

In the large open floor area in the middle, in front of the bar, four small chunky adventurers stand together with
room to spare: an armored knight with a red cape and shield, a viking with a two-handed axe, a mage with a pointed
purple hat and staff, a hooded ranger with a bow. Wide clear walkways between the door, the bar, the stage and the
stairs. Warm festive lighting, hanging wooden chandeliers with candles, garlands of small flags.
