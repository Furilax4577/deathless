Strict top-down orthographic view, camera looking straight down, like a game level map, north at the top of the
image, the whole scene fits in a square with margins.

Exact layout. In the exact center: a small three-step octagonal grey stone plateau with a glowing emerald green
crystal on a dark stone spire. Along the entire top edge (north): a wide band of massive grey faceted rock cliff,
impassable. In the middle of that cliff, straight above the center: a narrow waterfall falling into a small round
blue pool at the foot of the cliff. In the cliff just to the left of the waterfall: the dark mouth of a shallow
cave with a green glow spilling out of it, and a swirling green portal visible inside.

From the pool, a single calm flat blue river, about as wide as a small house, flows down, passes close to the right
(east) side of the central plateau, then curves and leaves the image at the bottom left corner (south-west). Exactly
two small wooden plank bridges cross the river, one upper right of the plateau and one below the plateau, and
between them one shallow ford of flat pale pebbles in lighter blue water.

Exactly six cottages in a loose ring around the plateau at medium distance, three on the left side of the river and
three on the right side, each with its door facing the plateau, none of them directly above the plateau so that the
view from the plateau to the waterfall stays open. Pale flagstone paths run from each door to the plateau, to both
bridges and to the cave.

Between the cliff and the cottages: a strip of scree with big grey boulders. Right side of the image (east): open
heath, pale yellow-brown ground, scattered rocks and tree stumps, no trees. Bottom (south) and left (west): sparse
forest with wide gaps of grass between the trees, tall trees seen from above, round clustered canopies and pointed conifers in four distinct greens, with
wide empty corridors. Three small round bare-earth clearings far from the village, one at the far right, one at the
far bottom, one at the far left, each connected to the village by an open corridor without trees.
