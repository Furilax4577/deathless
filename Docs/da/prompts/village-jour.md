High three-quarter view from the south, looking north. In the center of a grassy clearing: a glowing emerald green
crystal floating above a dark stone spire on a three-step octagonal grey stone plateau paved with large flat slabs.

In the background, filling the top of the image: a massive impassable cliff of big faceted grey boulders in three
shades of grey. In its middle, straight behind the crystal, a narrow waterfall falls into a small round pool. Just
to the left of the waterfall, at the foot of the cliff, the wide mouth of a shallow cave framed by big angular
boulders, with a swirling emerald green portal inside and a soft green glow spilling onto the ground.

From that pool a single calm flat blue river flows toward the viewer, passes close to the right side of the plateau,
then curves in front of it and leaves the image at the bottom left; the river is continuous from the pool to the
bottom left corner.
Two small wooden plank bridges with plain stone abutments cross it, and one shallow ford of flat pebbles.

Six cottages in a loose ring around the plateau, three on each side of the river, doors facing the crystal, the
line of sight from the crystal to the waterfall left open. Each shows its trade with two or three big readable
features: a two-storey tavern with double doors, a porch roof, a hanging mug sign and barrels; a blacksmith with an
open lean-to, a big stone chimney and a glowing orange forge; a two-storey tinkerer's shop with a large shop
window and a gear sign; a herbalist's cottage with ivy and flower boxes; a wizard's house with a round corner
tower, a dark blue conical slate roof and a round window; a plain cottage with a fenced vegetable garden and a well.

Pale flagstone paths from each door to the plateau, to the bridges and to the cave. Small lantern posts along the
paths. Scree at the foot of the cliff, open heath with stumps and rocks on the right, sparse forest on the left and
in the foreground: tall trees with bare trunks, layered conifers and round clustered canopies in four distinct
greens. Bright daylight, blue sky, a few puffy low-poly clouds.
