# Taverne, vue d'ambiance à hauteur de joueur (caméra à la troisième personne), depuis l'entrée.
base: _style-jeu
format: 16:9
eviter: first-person view, dark gloomy mood, green light, crowd of many people

Third-person game camera just inside the wide double doors of a large medieval tavern, behind and slightly above a
small chunky armored knight with a red cape seen from the back. He looks into a big warm hall with a high ceiling of
thick wooden beams, hanging wooden chandeliers with candles and garlands of small flags.

Straight ahead at the back, on the right: a long wooden bar counter with big barrels on tap behind it; the
innkeeper, a cheerful sturdy woman in a Bavarian dirndl dress with braided blonde hair, raises a beer mug. Above the
bar, a wooden gallery with a balustrade, and a straight staircase on the right wall. On the left wall a large stone
fireplace with a warm orange fire; in the back left corner a low wooden stage where a bard with a feathered hat
plays the lute. Near the fireplace a scruffy ragged hobo with a patched coat dozes on a bench with a bottle. A long
table with benches on the left, round tables with stools on the right. A wide empty plank floor in the middle, with
a viking, a mage with a pointed hat and a hooded ranger standing there, plenty of room around them. Warm festive
light.
