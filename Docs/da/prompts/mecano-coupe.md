Cutaway dollhouse view of the inside of a single medieval tinkerer shop, roof and front wall removed, seen from a
high three-quarter angle, the whole room visible, plain neutral background around it.

One rectangular shop room with a high ceiling of thick wooden beams, plank floor, cream plaster walls with dark
timber frame. In the near wall, a wide arched door and beside it a large shop window with small panes. At the back,
a wooden storage loft with a balustrade and a ladder, stacked with crates.

Against the back wall on the right: a long wooden shop counter. Behind it stands the shopkeeper, a stocky engineer
with brass goggles on his forehead, a leather apron and a big wrench. Behind him, wall racks displaying swords,
axes, bows, crossbows and shields. On the left wall: a big heavy workbench with a vise, gears, cog wheels and
springs, and a strange brass and wood contraption with large cog wheels and a bent metal stove pipe going up
through the ceiling. Crates and barrels of parts in the corners, a few cog wheels hanging from the beams.

In the large open floor area in front of the counter, four chunky big-headed adventurers stand side by side with
room to spare: an armored knight with a red cape and shield, a viking with a two-handed axe, a mage with a pointed
purple hat and staff, a hooded ranger with a bow. Wide clear walkway from the door to the counter. Warm daylight
and lantern light.
