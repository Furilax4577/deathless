# fenêtres et lanternes ; la nuit le portail est fermé, la grotte ne garde qu'une braise verte.

High three-quarter view from the south, looking north, deep blue night. In the center of a dark grassy clearing: a
glowing emerald green crystal floating above a dark stone spire on a three-step octagonal stone plateau. The
crystal is by far the brightest light in the image and casts an emerald glow on the plateau, the nearby grass and
the rippling river.

In the background a massive dark cliff of big faceted boulders, a narrow waterfall faintly visible straight behind
the crystal, and to the left of the waterfall the dark mouth of a shallow cave with only a faint dim green ember
deep inside.

A calm dark river runs along the right side of the plateau and leaves toward the bottom left, crossed by two small
wooden plank bridges and a shallow ford.

Six cottages in a loose ring around the plateau, three on each side of the river: a two-storey tavern with double
doors and a hanging mug sign, a blacksmith with an open lean-to and a glowing orange forge, a two-storey tinkerer's
shop with a lit shop window, a herbalist's cottage with ivy, a wizard's house with a round corner tower and a
softly lit round window, a plain cottage with a well. Warm orange windows, small warm lanterns on posts along the
flagstone paths and at the bridges, thin low ground mist, a few fireflies. Sparse dark forest on the left and in the
foreground.
