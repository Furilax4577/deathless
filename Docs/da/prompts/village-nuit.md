# Village refondu, même plan, de nuit (brief du 28/09/2026). La relique est la source de lumière principale.
format: 16:9
style: Stylized low-poly fantasy village for a co-op action game, seen from a high three-quarter view. Flat-shaded faceted geometry, no textures, soft color gradients, chunky toy-like proportions, clean readable shapes
eviter: photorealism, detailed textures, thin realistic proportions, dense forest everywhere, castle walls, modern objects, text, UI, bright moon

At the center of a round grassy clearing: a glowing emerald green crystal relic floating above a dark stone spire,
standing on a three-step octagonal stone plateau.

To the north, a massive impassable rocky cliff made of big faceted boulders, with a small waterfall falling into a
round pool. From the pool a calm flat river runs past the east side of the plateau and leaves toward the south-west.
Two small wooden plank bridges with stone abutments cross it, plus a shallow ford of flat pebbles.

Six half-timbered cottages in a wide ring around the relic, three on each bank, each facing the relic, red clay tile
roofs, arched plank doors framed by grey stone blocks, low smooth stone plinth: a tavern with a hanging mug sign and
barrels, a blacksmith with an open lean-to and glowing forge, a tinkerer's shop with a display window, a herbalist's
house covered in ivy, a wizard's house with a corner turret and a glowing round window.

Deep blue night. The green crystal is the main light source, casting an emerald glow on the plateau and rippling on
the river. Warm orange windows and small lanterns along the flagstone paths and bridges, low ground mist, a few
fireflies, the waterfall faintly visible against the dark cliff. Sparse forest to the south and west.
