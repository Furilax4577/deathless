Cutaway dollhouse view of a single small medieval cottage with its little garden, roof and front wall of the
cottage removed, seen from a high three-quarter angle, plain neutral background around it.

One simple rectangular room with a ceiling of thick wooden beams, plank floor, cream plaster walls with dark timber
frame, one window with shutters per wall. An arched plank door in the middle of the near wall. Inside: a stone
fireplace with a small orange fire and a cooking pot on the left wall, a sturdy table with two benches in the
middle, a bed with a patchwork blanket in the back right corner, a wooden chest, a cupboard with plates, a broom, a
rug. A villager woman with an apron stands by the table.

Outside, on the right side of the cottage on flat grass: a round stone well with a small wooden roof and a bucket,
a small fenced vegetable garden with rows of cabbages, carrots and pumpkins, a clothesline with white linen, a
stack of firewood against the wall.

On the open floor between the door and the table, four chunky big-headed adventurers stand side by side with room
to spare: an armored knight with a red cape and shield, a viking with a two-handed axe, a mage with a pointed purple
hat and staff, a hooded ranger with a bow. Warm daylight.
