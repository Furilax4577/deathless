# Village refondu, à hauteur de joueur depuis le pont sud (caméra à la troisième personne : jamais de vue FPS).

Third-person game camera placed behind and slightly above a small chunky armored knight with a red cape, seen from
the back, standing on a small wooden plank bridge. He looks across a narrow calm flat blue river, only as wide as the bridge is long, toward the far bank: there, on dry
grass well away from the water, a glowing emerald green crystal floats above a dark stone spire on a three-step
octagonal stone plateau. The plateau is on land, never in the water, and the river is a river, not a lake. Half-timbered cottages on
both banks, pale flagstone paths, small lantern posts. In the background a massive cliff of big faceted grey
boulders with a narrow waterfall straight behind the crystal and, to the left of the waterfall, the mouth of a
shallow cave glowing green. A few tall trees with bare trunks and round clustered canopies on the sides. Bright
daylight.
