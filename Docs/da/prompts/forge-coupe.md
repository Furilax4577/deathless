Cutaway dollhouse view of the inside of a single medieval blacksmith forge, roof and front wall removed, seen from
a high three-quarter angle, the whole building visible, plain neutral background around it.

One rectangular workshop room with a high ceiling of thick wooden beams, stone slab floor, cream plaster walls with
dark timber frame, barred windows. On its right side, an open lean-to shed with a plank roof on thick posts and no
walls. A wide arched door in the middle of the near wall.

Against the wall between the room and the lean-to: a massive stone forge hearth with a big hood and chimney, glowing
orange coals and flames, a large leather bellows beside it. In the middle of the room, on a thick log block: a big
iron anvil. At the anvil stands the blacksmith, a huge muscular bearded man, bare-headed, leather apron, raising a
hammer. Next to him a wooden quenching barrel full of water and a grindstone wheel. On the back wall: racks of
swords, axes and shields, and a long heavy workbench with tongs and hammers. Under the lean-to: a pile of coal,
stacked logs, a second small anvil, a cart wheel.

In the large open floor area in front of the anvil, four chunky big-headed adventurers stand side by side with room
to spare: an armored knight with a red cape and shield, a viking with a two-handed axe, a mage with a pointed purple
hat and staff, a hooded ranger with a bow. Wide clear walkway from the door to the anvil. Warm orange forge light.
