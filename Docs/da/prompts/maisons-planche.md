# Planche des six maisons du village (fiches validées par Quentin le 27/09/2026, étages ajoutés le 28/09/2026).
base: _style-jeu
format: 16:9
eviter: village background, river, characters

Model sheet of six separate cottages standing in two rows of three on a plain flat pale green ground with a plain
light sky, each seen from the front at a slight three-quarter angle, evenly spaced, same scale, a small chunky
knight figure next to the first one for scale (the doors are one and a half times his height).

All six share the same grammar: low smooth plain stone slab with two steps, cream or pale plaster walls, dark
red-brown timber frame, one window with shutters per wall, arched plank door framed by large plain grey stone
blocks, clay tile roof with big tiles, stone chimney. Each one differs by two or three big features:

1. Tavern: two storeys, wide double doors, deep porch roof over the entrance, hanging wooden sign with a mug, two
barrels and a bench, massive chimney.
2. Blacksmith: one storey, open lean-to on the side with a glowing orange forge, big stone chimney, anvil, stacked
logs, barred windows without shutters.
3. Tinkerer's shop: two storeys, large shop window with small panes, hanging sign with a gear and a wrench, bent
metal stove pipe, a cog wheel weathervane, crates.
4. Herbalist: one storey, steeper mossy roof, ivy and flowers on the timber frame, flower boxes, herbs drying under
a small porch, hanging sign with an amber potion flask (never green).
5. Wizard: a one-storey half-timbered cottage like the others, with a round tower attached at one corner, dark blue conical slate roof, round window, crescent moon
weathervane, pale ochre plaster.
6. Plain cottage: one storey, small fenced vegetable garden, a stone well, a clothesline, stacked firewood.
